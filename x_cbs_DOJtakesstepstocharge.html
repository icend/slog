DOJ takes steps to charge James Comey again.
The Justice Department took steps to charge James Comey with leaking classified information earlier this month, court records show, in its third attempt to bring criminal charges against the former FBI director and Trump foe.
​
Prosecutors are seeking to charge Comey with two counts of violating the Espionage Act by transmitting national defense information to a person not entitled to receive it. He has not been indicted by a grand jury or charged by criminal complaint, and was instead targeted through a criminal information filing.
​
In this case, the information was filed in order to preserve the statute of limitations before it expires, according to a Justice Department spokesperson. The official added that "this is an ongoing criminal investigation, and we will not comment further."
​
A criminal information does not require approval by a grand jury, or a finding by a magistrate judge of probable cause that there is evidence to establish that a crime was committed.
​
Comey's attorney, Patrick Fitzgerald, said Comey "steadfastly maintains his innocence."
​
The latest effort by the Justice Department stems from allegations that Comey gave national defense information to an unnamed individual for disclosure in a newspaper in October 2016.
​
According to court filings in the case, The New York Times published an article on Oct. 5, 2016, titled, "Yahoo Said to Have Aided U.S. Email Surveillance By Adapting Spam Filter." The article cited two unnamed government officials who spoke on the condition of anonymity.

司法部采取措施再次起诉詹姆斯·科米。
法庭记录显示，司法部本月早些时候采取措施指控詹姆斯·科米（James Comey）泄露机密信息，这是司法部第三次试图对这位前联邦调查局局长、特朗普的对手提起刑事指控。
​
检察官正试图以两项违反《反间谍法》的罪名起诉科米，罪名是将国防信息传递给无权接收的人。他没有受到大陪审团的起诉，也没有受到刑事诉讼的指控，而是通过刑事信息档案被针对。
​
司法部发言人说，在本案中，提交这些信息是为了在诉讼时效到期前保留这些信息。这名官员补充说，“这是一项正在进行的刑事调查，我们不会进一步置评。”
​
刑事信息不需要大陪审团的批准，也不需要地方法官对可能原因的发现，即有证据证明犯罪已经发生。
​
科米的律师帕特里克·菲茨杰拉德（Patrick Fitzgerald）表示，科米“坚定地坚持自己的清白”。
​
司法部的最新努力源于科米于2016年10月将国防信息交给一位未具名人士，让其在一家报纸上披露的指控。
​
根据该案件的法庭文件，《纽约时报》2016年10月5日发表了一篇题为《雅虎称通过调整垃圾邮件过滤器帮助美国监控电子邮件》的文章。这篇文章援引了两名不愿透露姓名的政府官员的话。
